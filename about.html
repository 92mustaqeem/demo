About the College

GOVT. MODEL COLLEGE (EVENING) MIRPURKHAS

History

Government Model College Mirpurkhas (Evening) has a long and distinguished history of providing accessible higher education to the people of Mirpurkhas and surrounding areas.

The institution was established in 1962 in Mirpurkhas by the Tharparkar Welfare Association as a private college. From its very beginning, the college was established with the aim of providing educational opportunities to students who wished to pursue higher education while continuing their employment and other responsibilities.

In 1972, the college was nationalized by the Government of Pakistan/Sindh and became a government institution. Since then, it has continued to serve the educational needs of the community as an Arts and Commerce college offering evening classes.

The evening character of the college has been one of its distinctive features. It was particularly established to provide higher education opportunities to working students, enabling them to attend classes after their working hours. Over the decades, thousands of students have benefited from this flexible educational arrangement.

The college has especially contributed to the advancement of Commerce and Arts education in the region. Many students enrolled in programs such as B.Com and B.A. have successfully completed their education while working in different departments, institutions and organizations. Recently, the BS Commerce and BS Urdu programs have also been running successfully at the college.

The alumni of Government Model College Mirpurkhas are serving in different parts of Pakistan and in various fields of public and private life. Their professional contributions reflect the important role played by the college in promoting education, employment and social development.

With its rich history, commitment to working students, and continuing contribution to higher education, Government Model College Mirpurkhas (Evening) remains an important educational institution of Mirpurkhas and the surrounding region.

Mission

Providing higher education opportunities to working students

Offering

Provides evening shift classes in commerce and arts disciplines, including Associate Degree (AD) and Bachelor of Science (BS) programs (e.g., BS Commerce batches).
